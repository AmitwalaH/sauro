question: What is the current status of the EU AI Act?
---
The EU AI Act is in force and largely being enforced, but its high-risk rules were pushed back by an amending law this summer.

The amendment (the “AI Omnibus”)
The Commission proposed targeted amendments to the AI Act in November 2025 as part of its digital simplification package; they were adopted in June 2026 and entered into force on 27 July 2026. The law is Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, published in the Official Journal on July 24, 2026. It went through a provisional trilogue agreement on May 7, 2026, a European Parliament vote on June 16, and Council adoption on June 29.

What applies now

Bans and transparency: Bans have applied since February 2025, and transparency duties since August 2026. The Omnibus did not postpone the general transparency obligations in Article 50. In practice that means making chatbots recognisable as AI and labelling deepfakes.

Enforcement: Since 2 August 2026, the AI Office and national authorities have been enforcing the Act.

General-purpose AI models: these obligations have applied since August 2025 (from my own knowledge; the sources above don’t cover them).
What’s coming

2 December 2026 (in about two weeks): A full EU-wide ban takes effect on AI systems built mainly to generate non-consensual intimate images, such as “nudification” apps. Companies offering them must comply by that date. The prohibition also covers AI used to generate CSAM.

How big a change is this?
Commentators mostly see it as a delay rather than a rollback. Gibson Dunn calls it a deferral, not a dismantling, since the law’s structure and core obligations remain. There were some substantive changes, though: the AI literacy obligation was softened, and the new prohibitions were added. 
hellogrowth

Still pending: The wider Digital Omnibus also proposes changes to the GDPR, ePrivacy rules and the Data Act. That part is still a proposal at first reading and is the most contested, with privacy and digital-rights groups warning it could weaken key protections.
