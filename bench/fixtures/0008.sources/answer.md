India’s current GDP growth forecast for FY 2026–27 is about 7.0–7.1%. The Reserve Bank of India projects 7.1%, while the World Bank and OECD also forecast 7.1%; Moody’s recently revised its estimate to 7.0%.

Latest forecasts
Forecaster	FY 2026–27 real GDP growth
Reserve Bank of India	7.1% 
World Bank	7.1% 
OECD	7.1% 
Moody’s Ratings	7.0% 
Why forecasts rose
Forecasters upgraded India’s outlook after strong first-quarter growth of 7.8%, resilient domestic demand, improving investment, and export strength.

The main risks are inflation and possible tighter monetary policy, which could weigh on private investment.
